Privacy Policy

Quiz Shorts Publisher · Effective 23 September 2026

Quiz Shorts Publisher ("the Tool") is operated by Numble ("we", "us"). The Tool publishes our own short quiz videos to TikTok accounts that we own. This policy explains what information the Tool handles and how.

1. Information we access through TikTok

When we connect a TikTok account through TikTok's Login Kit, TikTok provides an access token, a refresh token, an open_id, and basic profile information (display name, username, avatar). With the video.publish permission, the Tool also reads the account's posting settings (available privacy levels, whether comments, Duets and Stitches are enabled, and the maximum video length) in order to display them before publishing.

2. How we use it

  • To show which account a video will be posted to.
  • To publish videos, captions and the settings we choose (privacy level, comment/Duet/Stitch permissions, content disclosure) to that account.
  • To check the processing status of a post.

We do not use the information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored in a local configuration file on our own computer, readable only by our user account. They are not uploaded to any server, shared with third parties, or embedded in any public code.

4. Third parties

The Tool sends video files, captions and settings to TikTok's API in order to publish them. TikTok's handling of that content is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy. We do not send TikTok data to any other service.

5. Other users

The Tool is used only by us, for accounts we own. It does not collect any information from viewers of our videos or from any other TikTok user.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until we disconnect the account, which deletes the local configuration file, or until they expire. You can also revoke the Tool's access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at numworks.contact@gmail.com.
