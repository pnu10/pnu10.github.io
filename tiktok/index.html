Quiz Shorts Publisher

A small publishing tool by Numble for our own short-form quiz videos.

Quiz Shorts Publisher is an internal tool we use to publish our daily "would you rather" quiz videos (Dutch channel Wat kies jij? and German channel Würdest du eher?) to our own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Shows the video and lets us review and edit the caption before publishing.
  • Lets us choose who can view the video and whether comments, Duets and Stitches are allowed, using the options TikTok reports for the account.
  • Publishes the video to the connected TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API and shows processing status.

Data

The tool runs on our own computer. It stores only the TikTok access tokens for our own accounts. It does not collect data from any other user. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
